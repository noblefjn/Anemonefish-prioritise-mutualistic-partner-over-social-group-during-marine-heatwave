# Anemonefish-prioritise-mutualistic-partner-over-social-group-during-marine-heatwave
Database, code and figures relating to the article: Anemonefish prioritise mutualistic partner over social group during marine heatwave 

This repository consists of multiple data files relating the the research article "Anemonefish prioritise mutualistic partner over social group during marine heatwave". These consist of the dataframe and code used to analyse the data and produce the models and figures present in the article. The full output of each model, are presented here for examination, as referred to within the manuscript of the article. 

Here is the list of files present in the repository and the data they contain: 

Anemonefish_prioritise_mutualistic_partner_over_social_group_during_marine_heatwave-Supplementary_Materials.docx
- Supplementary data tables which have been referred to in the main manuscript. Consisting of full ethogram, model outputs and posthoc testing.

Article Dataset
- Dataset used for analysis. Not all factors utilised in final analysis but listed here:
-   Observation ID - Unique identifier for each observation
-   Behaviour - Specific behaviour recorded
-   Subject - Rank level of individual fish within the recorded social group
-   Total Duration - Length of video footage analysed
-   Time_vis - Time of fish visible
-   Anemone - Unique tag identifier for the anemone
-   Reef - Name of reef
-   Bleached - Bleached Status of the anemone during the observation
-   Heatwave - At what stage of the study the observation occurred
-     Before = at peak of heatwave before bleaching (Time Point 1)
-     During = after peak of heatwave during bleaching event (Time Point 2)
- Date - Date the observation occurred
- Breeding.Non.Breeding - Breeding status of the anemonefish group
- Behavioural Category - Overarching behavioural category of the behaviour
- Behaviours per second - rate behaviours occurred during the observation
- Count - Count of the behaviour during the observation
- N_fish - Count of fish within the group
- Bleached Overall - Bleached status of the anemone during the bleaching event (Timepoint 2)
- Anemone Area - Area of the anemone's oral disc
- Day Temp - Temperature recorded on the day of observation at the anemone
